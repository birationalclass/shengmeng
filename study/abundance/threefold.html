Nef Abundance: Threefold Behavior

Smooth projective complex threefolds with KX ∼ 0 and q(X) = 0.

Theorem. Let X be a smooth projective threefold over ℂ with KX ∼ 0 and q(X) = 0. Let D be a nef Cartier divisor satisfying n(X, D) = 3 and D³ = 0. Then H⁰(X, mD) ≠ 0 for some positive integer m.

Source-based proof draft for joint study. The geometric arguments and nonvanishing deduction are written out with explicit sources; correctness has not been independently verified.
