Numerical Semiampleness of Nef Adjoint Divisors

Let $(X,B)$ be a projective klt $\Q$-pair over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Suppose that $K_X+B$ is pseudo-effective, and let $M$ be a nef $\Q$-Cartier divisor on $X$. If \[ D=K_X+B+M \] is nef, then there is a semiample $\Q$-Cartier divisor $L$ on $X$ such that $D\equiv L$.

记号：原文 B 为有效边界，M 为 nef 除子。下方 K3 讨论中的 B 对应原文 M。英文陈述与公式保留原文。
