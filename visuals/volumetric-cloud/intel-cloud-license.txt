Source and changes

Source: https://dpel.aswf.io/intel-cloud-library/
License: https://dpel.aswf.io/intel-cloud-library/intel-volumetric-clouds-library/

This redistributes the active bounds of the density grid from intelCloudLib_dense.0.S.vdb in the Intel® Volumetric Clouds Library small archive. The source 163 × 96 × 136 grid was downsampled with a voxel-area filter to 156 × 92 × 130, padded with two transparent voxels on each side, and encoded as a 160 × 96 × 134 unsigned byte grid. The original density scale [0, 0.5] maps linearly to [0, 255]. The x axis is fastest and y is up. The original archive, grid transform, and auxiliary metadata are available from the source.
