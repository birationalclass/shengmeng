# 数学难民营 — A retreat for wandering minds

An original, navigable 3D architectural scene for Sheng Meng's Visual Lab. **Not a video, image panorama, or 2.5D photo displacement.** The structure, rooms, furniture, plants, pool and surrounding terrain are modeled geometry. This is an initial reference-inspired scene, not a verified pixel-equivalent reconstruction of the supplied film.

## Reference and camera design

Reference: [梦境之初q — 刷到就说明你未来将会拥有这样的房子](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1umun6PEvs/), 8:09, posted 2026-08-06, labelled as including AI-generated content by Bilibili. Initial access returned 412; a later ordinary browser opening succeeded. Observed opening material includes a layered dark-framed glass hillside villa, terraces, a reflecting-water passage with stone steps, warm linear lighting, an atrium and window-side interiors. We have **not** extracted the original camera tracks, underlying models or material maps, nor verified every shot of the full film. No original frames, audio or models are redistributed.

The eight-chapter tour uses arc-length sampled Catmull–Rom camera and target curves with eased movement: coastal arrival, low pool-side dolly, library, auditorium, a dedicated chalkboard lesson, ocean terrace, rising retreat and a bamboo/creek/waterfall garden chapter. The auditorium camera follows the central aisle rather than crossing seats. The lesson camera follows the active board pair with a damped, level close view. Each chapter has its own position, target and field of view. Chapter transitions fade, manual dragging stops the tour, and resume blends back (long relocations fade rather than fly through walls). These are inspired camera categories, **not motion capture from the reference**.

## Approved peninsula layout — 2026-09-20

This implementation follows layout sketch 02, with an **open-air central water court**. It is a layout/model revision, not a completed architectural engineering design.

- Cardinal directions are explicit: east is +X, north is −Z. Land connects from the west; north, east and south open onto the ocean. Mountains, woodland, the creek and waterfall stay landward. No offshore islands, boats or ornamental objects interrupt the eastward horizon. The sun remains due east as the light-height control changes.
- Two low, transparent main-villa wings flank the water court. They contain an academic living room and discussion room, with smaller upper studies/seminar rooms and terraces. A detached quiet library, two residential villas and a service/tea-kitchen pavilion sit farther west. One small timber tea pavilion provides a classical accent.
- A single connected pool surrounds the dry villa platforms, traverses the open central court, then extends along both sides of the offshore hall to a due-east infinity edge. Its disjoint surface cells cover 952 plan-square units, or 1,904 world-square units. Real basin bottoms, side walls, coping, an overflow sheet and recovery trough distinguish the pool from seawater. This is rendered geometry, not fluid dynamics or a swimming-pool engineering specification.
- Three shallow arch bridges have modeled timber decks and curved rails; separate level companion crossings keep the route usable without climbing the arch. Code checks reject ground-floor slabs covering swimming lanes except those explicit crossings. No accessibility certification is claimed.
- The low **32-seat offshore seminar hall** has a 3.2 m clear height, four rows, two seat blocks, a central aisle and a small lectern. Thin pale seat shells, sage cushions, metal supports and writing tablets replace the previous oversized chairs. Listeners face east; the six blackboards stand at the eastern teaching front and face west. A separate operable blackout shade controls glare behind the boards. The structure is on supported platforms above actual seawater, not filled land.
- Site coordinates retain the existing √2 scale, while room clear heights and furniture use physical dimensions. The legacy board assembly is independently scaled and rotated into the compact hall. All board-focus cameras use its transformed normal. The original notes, six-board alternation, chalk wear/dust and damp wiping remain intact.
- Paths, roofs, glazing gaps, stairs, furniture and books are modeled geometry. Room functions are blocked out; detailed services, engineering and every interior fixture are not complete. Free observation has no collision-controlled walking physics and can pass through geometry.

## Rendering and environment

- Rasterized PBR wood/stone surfaces, physical glass, procedural cloth/metal microdetail, PMREM sky lighting, cached shadows, ACES tone mapping and restrained bloom. Rounded furniture retains world-sized bevels. There is no path tracing, displacement tessellation or photogrammetric building reconstruction.
- One 512² planar-reflection pass serves the entire connected pool. The 8,000-unit ocean uses analytic waves/normals, Fresnel horizon color and sunlight, avoiding another reflection camera. Its surface remains planar. A 128² two-dimensional depth lookup derived from the actual terrain handles all three coastlines rather than assuming a single east-facing cliff.
- A continuous ridged height field with a fine near-site mesh replaces isolated cone mountains. Fine/coarse terrain boundaries match. Triplanar rocky slopes, photographic bark/ground/rock maps and atmospheric fog provide surface detail and distance layers.
- Instanced branching trees, folded leaves, palms, ferns, grasses and irregular rock outcrops remain. The west-side forest contains 200 trees; three giant trees, bamboo groves, lawns and flower borders frame the dry campus. Vegetation excludes buildings, pools, the carved creek and seawater. Leaf breeze is disabled for reduced motion; cached shadows do not track each moving leaf.
- The relocated landward watercourse descends through a waterfall and plunge pool, then a creek toward the sea. Terrain and water share one sampled centerline. Ribbon shading, local foam and a bounded mist particle system are effects, not hydrodynamic or volumetric simulation. The landscape uses sheltered landward placement and open sea views as composition, not scientifically established feng-shui effects.
- Geometry assembly currently counts **1,017,992 mesh triangles, 4,737 instances and 228 scene objects**, below the prior 1,285,708-triangle scene and the 1.3M code budget. This is a mesh-count reduction, **not measured FPS improvement**. Spatial instancing bounds draw batches; distant plants do not cast shadows. Site-distance foliage detail is not camera-adaptive LoD.
- No new photographic textures were downloaded. The six 1K landscape JPGs still total 5,921,427 bytes, roughly 32 MiB uncompressed RGBA with mipmaps. Mobile density/MSAA budgets and the independent vector chalk reader remain unchanged. GPU frame rate/memory and visual quality have not been measured in this iteration.
- Fullscreen, hideable controls, tour/light/sensitivity settings and reduced-motion support remain. Mouse rotation defaults to 22% of the original OrbitControls gain; single-touch uses 16.5%. Manual camera ownership is unchanged. No autoplay audio.
- Entrance signs read “数学难民营”. Separate NS and Hodge blackboards remain in the academic living villa, including the fractured translucent NS background. They state the mathematical questions rather than announcing solutions. Formulations: [Clay NS statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/navierstokes.pdf), [Clay Hodge conjecture](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/hodge-conjecture/).

## Six-board spectral-sequence classroom

The auditorium has three independent pairs of counter-moving boards, six surfaces total, each with its own canvas texture. Two depth-separated rails let the boards pass while exchanging upper/lower positions. The sequence writes across the three lower boards, lowers the other boards in turn and preserves the previous work above. Grain stays deterministic rather than flickering. Row-wise reveal preserves real SVG mathematical layout; it is a chalk-writing effect, **not reconstructed human handwriting strokes**.

The chalk tip now samples actual SVG ink columns, lifts over gaps/row changes and stays at the visible reveal frontier. If canvas pixel readback is unavailable it falls back to row-based motion. The shaft wears from 17 cm down to 4.5 cm while the contact tip remains anchored; short pieces are replaced between pages. At most 64 subtle falling-powder particles are recycled. A tilted felt-faced eraser makes fourteen eased horizontal sweeps with small vertical variation and lifted turns over 24 seconds; the texture removal uses the same footprint/trajectory as the 3D tool, with a coverage test across the whole board. A single incremental wipe cache avoids replaying all past clipping operations every frame. Recently wiped regions darken slightly and have a local low-resolution roughness-map change, then dry out over 12 seconds. Pause freezes wear/dust/drying; reduced-motion hides tools/powder and clears wet trails. Only changing boards upload color/roughness textures. The six 384 × 160 roughness maps add about 1.9 MiB including mipmaps. These are visual effects rather than simulated chalk adhesion or moisture physics.

`build-chalk-notes.mjs` reads the existing `study/spectral` exports and converts their formulas into 65 local SVG pages using build-only MathJax 3.2.2. Pages cover double complexes, filtrations, convergence, Hodge and Leray; the manifest retains the source TeX and section. They reproduce the exported main formula content with short context, not every proof-dialog derivation or interactive diagram. The classroom links back to the original notebook for full statements, proofs and interactions. No runtime CDN is needed. SVG formula paths avoid depending on installed mathematical fonts; Chinese labels use system Kai-style/Chinese fallbacks. See [MathJax server integration](https://docs.mathjax.org/en/v3.2/server/direct.html).

Controls: select any page, previous/next, pause/resume writing, erase/rewrite, focus the lesson camera, toggle the sea-facing blackout shade, and slide or exchange each board pair. Manual lifting pauses writing. Reduced-motion mode displays complete static pages; page controls and direct lifts still work. Hidden tabs do not advance the lesson. Loading failures appear in the classroom status.

### Mobile clarity and rendering budget

Phones no longer automatically select the old 1×, no-MSAA preset. Fine quality allows native pixel density up to 3×, bounded by a 3.5-million-pixel small-screen budget (6 million on larger screens). A 390 × 844 CSS-pixel, 3× device stays at 3× in either orientation. On compact screens with a multisampled native framebuffer, the scene renders directly with native antialiasing and ACES, bypassing bloom/composer passes. If native multisampling is unavailable, the compositor uses hardware-limited MSAA. Flow mode remains an explicit choice; no device frame-rate guarantee is implied. Resize/orientation changes re-evaluate the budget and dispose outdated MSAA attachments. Compact shadow maps use 1024²; readable pixels take priority over bloom.

Portrait teaching frames the active board rather than retreating to fit the full pair. Board headings/body text increase to 58/42 texture pixels, short formulas have a consistent scale cap, grain is less intrusive, and mipmaps reduce minification shimmer. Only actually changed board textures upload. The optional **高清板书** layer presents local standalone SVG equations and reflowed HTML explanations, independent of the WebGL texture and postprocessing. It opens on small-screen entry to the teaching chapter; closing it is respected for that chapter. Formula size is adjustable (18–36 CSS pixels); long formulas scroll horizontally rather than shrinking illegibly. It shows a complete page while the 3D classroom retains its writing/erasing animation. Full notebook text is retained in this reader even when the physical board uses a shorter text excerpt.

To regenerate with the pinned extracted `mathjax-full` package: `node visuals/math-refuge/build-chalk-notes.mjs /path/to/mathjax-full-3.2.2`. MathJax's license is included under `assets/chalk/`.

### Contemporary techniques considered

Primary documentation reviewed on 2026-09-19:

- [Three.js WebGPURenderer](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGPURenderer.html): modern WebGPU backend with WebGL2 fallback. Not enabled here: this project currently shares pinned Three.js 0.180.0 WebGL addons and planar-reflection materials. Calling this module WebGPU-rendered would be incorrect.
- [Spark Gaussian splat LoD](https://sparkjs.dev/docs/lod-getting-started/): streamed/adaptive splat rendering can support detailed captured environments. No training-ready, geometrically consistent capture or splat asset was supplied, so Gaussian splatting is not used or simulated by ordinary points.
- [three-gpu-pathtracer](https://github.com/gkjohnson/three-gpu-pathtracer): progressive physically based path tracing. Not included in this version: convergence during continuous travel and target-device performance require a separate validation pass. Current lighting is rasterized PBR with planar reflection, not full path tracing/global illumination.

The requested goal “quality and detail no lower than the video” remains **unverified and not claimed achieved**. Matching every reference room and close-up would require more asset detail and visual comparison. The user explicitly selected code checks only for this iteration, so no browser interaction/visual acceptance is performed.

## Code verification

### Manual camera ownership

Canvas pointer-down takes over the camera in the document capture phase, before OrbitControls processes the gesture. Dragging, multi-touch, pointer cancellation and window blur cannot restart the tour. Drag-generated compatibility clicks on chapter/tour/board-focus controls are suppressed; a fresh button press is accepted immediately. Light/sensitivity sliders are not treated as camera drags. Orbit auto-rotation is explicitly disabled. Chapter buttons and board focus now select a stationary viewpoint instead of implicitly starting a tour. After manual takeover, only the tour button (or its deliberate Space-key shortcut) starts touring; held-key repeats are ignored. The initial page-load tour behavior is unchanged. Mode labels and canvas diagnostics update immediately on takeover.

Run `node --test visuals/math-refuge/checks.test.mjs`: **18 code checks** cover connected/nonoverlapping pool geometry, dry-room/basin separation, bridge landings, offshore foundations and an unobstructed eastern horizon, board orientation/ceiling fit, all eight camera chapters, local assets, finite model buffers and the 1.3M-triangle budget. They also cover terrain seams, creek clearance, forest placement, texture hashes, shader replacement hooks, 65 SVG pages, mobile pixel budgets, chalk/eraser motion, six-board controls and manual-camera ownership.

Scene assembly uses real Three.js geometry with mocked texture/PMREM/canvas/image services. The tests do not compile GPU shaders, render screenshots, measure frame rate or certify architectural safety. Browser/visual testing is intentionally omitted at the user's request. The reference informed the design but was not used for a full-film, side-by-side acceptance test.
