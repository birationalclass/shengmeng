# 数学难民营 — A retreat for wandering minds

An original, navigable 3D architectural scene for Sheng Meng's Visual Lab. **Not a video, image panorama, or 2.5D photo displacement.** The structure, rooms, furniture, plants, sea-access steps and distant islands are modeled geometry. This is an initial reference-inspired scene, not a verified pixel-equivalent reconstruction of the supplied film.

## Reference and camera design

Reference: [梦境之初q — 刷到就说明你未来将会拥有这样的房子](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1umun6PEvs/), 8:09, posted 2026-08-06, labelled as including AI-generated content by Bilibili. Initial access returned 412; a later ordinary browser opening succeeded. Observed opening material includes a layered dark-framed glass hillside villa, terraces, a reflecting-water passage with stone steps, warm linear lighting, an atrium and window-side interiors. We have **not** extracted the original camera tracks, underlying models or material maps, nor verified every shot of the full film. No original frames, audio or models are redistributed.

The eight-chapter tour uses arc-length sampled Catmull–Rom camera and target curves with eased movement: coastal arrival, courtyard dolly, library, auditorium, a dedicated chalkboard lesson, ocean terrace, rising retreat and a contained offshore garden chapter. The auditorium camera follows the central aisle rather than crossing seats. The lesson camera follows the active board pair with a damped, level close view. Each chapter has its own position, target and field of view. Normal chapter transitions fade, manual dragging stops the tour, and resume continuously blends back without a blackout. Free-view resume interpolation has no architectural collision avoidance. These are inspired camera categories, **not motion capture from the reference**.

## Expandable offshore campus — 2026-09-20

This revision follows the **fixed two-storey hall, arch bridges, north coffee cabin, shorter terrace and blue daytime sky** requests. Pools remain removed; seawater separates the connected platforms. It is a layout/model revision, not a completed architectural engineering design.

- Cardinal directions are explicit: east is +X, north is −Z. All buildings, service paths, gardens and expansion docks sit on modeled offshore platforms. All near-campus mountain, mainland and natural waterfall/creek geometry is removed. No support piles are generated. Decks stand about 1.3 m above mean sea level with continuous fascia extending underwater; planting survives only in the contained offshore garden trays and tree wells. Four islands occupy distant oblique views; the central due-east view has no islands or boats. Sunrise is east; the simulated sun moves west during the day.
- Two low, transparent main-villa wings flank the dry courtyard. They contain an academic living room and discussion room, with smaller upper studies/seminar rooms and terraces. A detached quiet library, two residential villas and a service/tea-kitchen pavilion sit farther west. One small timber tea pavilion provides a classical accent.
- The swimming pool remains removed. The east terrace is shortened to plan X=26–56, Z=−15–18 (990 plan-square units, down from 3,360); chaise lounges are removed. Two small sofa groups remain near its edges, leaving the east view open. Three shallow timber arch bridges cross genuine seawater gaps between the main wings, hall platform and new north coffee cabin. The cabin has a counter, modeled two-group espresso machine with cups and bean hoppers, seating and its own functional sign.
- Twelve broad seven-step stairways link the principal ground-floor terraces to the sea, with about 18 cm risers and 54 cm treads. Stair feet end 3.5 cm above mean sea level. The new bridges have slender side rails. This virtual layout is not a certified coastal or accessibility construction design.
- The **30-seat offshore seminar hall** restores its 20-plan-unit transverse width while increasing clear height to 4.9 m. Each of the three rows has ten seats, in two five-seat blocks; within-block seat centers are 1.2 plan units apart (previously 1.5). The front row moves back by 1 plan unit (about 1.41 m), leaving 4.95 m from its center to the writing plane. The three rows have physical rises of 0, 0.18 and 0.36 m from front to back, two seat blocks, a central carpeted stair aisle with 9 cm steps and a small lectern. Deep warm-gray curved wraparound shells, reclined smoke-brown upholstery, broad soft seat cushions, headrests, champagne supports and walnut writing tablets form a quieter lounge palette. Headrests share the same physical support curve as the upholstery, with a 5 mm separation and no sideways roll. The carpet covers both floor and stepped tiers, deepening from light taupe in front through medium taupe to darker taupe at the back; the north opaque acoustic wall is removed to preserve the left-hand sea view. Listeners face east; the six blackboards stand at the eastern teaching front and face west. Their linear dimensions grow by 20% (lecture scale 0.60 to 0.72); the lower writing edge is now 1.356 m above the front floor. Both upper boards and the backing/rails clear the raised roof. Physical control buttons remain at reachable heights rather than moving up with the writing surface. A separate operable blackout shade controls glare behind the boards. The structure is on supported platforms above actual seawater, not filled land.
- Site coordinates retain the existing √2 scale, while room clear heights and furniture use physical dimensions. The legacy board assembly is independently scaled and rotated into the compact hall. All board-focus cameras use its transformed normal. The blackboard bottom is at least 0.905 m above the floor at every lift position. The original notes, six-board alternation, chalk wear/dust and damp wiping remain intact.
- Paths, roofs, glazing gaps, stairs, furniture and books are modeled geometry. Room functions are blocked out; detailed services, engineering and every interior fixture are not complete. Free observation has no wall-collision walking physics and can pass through rooms. A shared water-height guard runs after orbit/dolly/pan, keyboard movement and automatic cameras: the eye stays at least 1 m above sea level, and the orbit target cannot sink below that surface. Looking up toward the roof is still allowed.

The enlarged hall terrace uses large-format, honed medium-dark gray-brown stone slabs with subtle generated mineral clouding, low-contrast 6 mm joints, a charcoal border and a single matte champagne entry threshold. It replaces the generic small-square tile grid. All intersecting architectural platforms are tessellated as nonoverlapping unions rather than stacking coplanar floors; generic fine grid lines and repeated platform-edge lights are removed. Camera near clipping increases from 0.08 to 0.2 m and shadow bias is adjusted. These are code-level anti-flicker measures; visual acceptance remains unperformed.

### Fixed two-storey hall, scene clock and planting

The retractable roof and its controls are removed. A fixed upper lounge with terrace and a 34-step external west staircase follows the main villa's two-storey language. Ground-floor clear height remains 4.9 m, preserving the raised boards and seats. East glazing beside the boards now uses four wide panes with three narrow joints. The scene follows device local time; a settings slider provides a temporary time preview. Room-specific warm fixtures remain; the hall now has a fixed matte acoustic ceiling with three linear diffusers. Twenty-one actual light sources serve the campus, including upper rooms, reading areas and dedicated blackboard lighting. No extra shadow maps or bloom are introduced. Environment refresh remains throttled to half-hour steps. Clear daytime sky uses turbidity 1.8, Rayleigh 2 and Mie coefficient 0.002; blue distance fog has density 0.00028 at noon, increasing slightly at twilight/night. This is a visual daylight model, not calibrated astronomy or lighting.

A transparent cyan touch display on the east glass switches Chinese/English. It uses brightness feedback without mechanical travel. Short presses activate it; camera drags do not.

Six framed specimen tree wells use small exposed soil apertures within stone borders. Layered Terminalia-inspired trees and white frangipani-inspired flowering trees supplement the existing larger garden trees; large roots have wider contained wells. The surrounding garden trays are green groundcover/paving rather than large bare-earth islands. Ten facility entrances have large, related mathematical knot sculptures in matte bronze on dark pedestals, offset from door openings and stairs. The lounge example is retained; the hall, discussion room, library, two residences, tea service, tea pavilion arrival gate and coffee cabin get companion pieces, with geometry shared among four knot types.

Only the arrival-gate logo says “数学难民营”. Individual door signs identify 报告厅, 学术客厅, 讨论室, 图书馆, 研究员居所, 茶室, 咖啡小屋 and 竹庭茶亭.

## Rendering and environment

- Rasterized PBR wood/stone surfaces, physical glass, procedural cloth/metal microdetail, PMREM sky lighting, cached shadows, ACES tone mapping and no bloom. Blackboard surfaces have zero emission, zero specular intensity and a muted chalk tint; button labels also use lit matte materials. Ocean glitter, glass specular and metal gloss are reduced. Rounded furniture retains world-sized bevels. There is no path tracing, displacement tessellation or photogrammetric building reconstruction.
- The pool's planar reflection camera is removed. The only water surface is the 8,000-unit analytic ocean, using the existing normal texture and subdued sun glitter; no new water framebuffer is allocated.
- Four lightweight, irregular green/rocky island meshes occupy distant oblique views; there is still no mainland or mountain mesh beneath the facilities. An unobstructed due-east cone remains for sunrise. The islands use original radial geometry and vertex colors, no downloaded imagery; atmospheric fog provides distance separation.
- Instanced branching trees, folded leaves, palms, ferns, grasses and irregular rock outcrops remain. Three framed giant trees, specimen trees, bamboo groves, lawns and flower borders remain on the garden platforms; the distant natural forest is removed. Contained garden vegetation excludes buildings and seawater; campus planting uses separate supported garden/planter surfaces above the seabed. Leaf breeze is disabled for reduced motion; cached shadows do not track each moving leaf.
- The old natural waterfall/creek model and its source data remain in the repository for possible reuse, but are not imported or created in the live scene.
- Geometry assembly currently counts **546,600 mesh triangles, 3,848 instances and 228 root scene objects**, below the prior 1,285,708-triangle scene and the 1.3M code budget. This is a mesh-count reduction, **not measured FPS improvement**. Spatial instancing bounds draw batches; distant plants do not cast shadows. Site-distance foliage detail is not camera-adaptive LoD.
- No new photographic textures were downloaded. The six 1K landscape JPGs still total 5,921,427 bytes, roughly 32 MiB uncompressed RGBA with mipmaps. Mobile density/MSAA budgets and the independent vector chalk reader remain unchanged. GPU frame rate/memory and visual quality have not been measured in this iteration.
- Fullscreen, hideable controls, tour/light/sensitivity settings and reduced-motion support remain. Mouse rotation defaults to 22% of the original OrbitControls gain; single-touch uses 16.5%. Manual camera ownership is unchanged. No autoplay audio.
- The single arrival logo reads “数学难民营”. Separate NS and Hodge blackboards remain in the academic living villa, including the fractured translucent NS background. They state the mathematical questions rather than announcing solutions. Formulations: [Clay NS statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/navierstokes.pdf), [Clay Hodge conjecture](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/hodge-conjecture/).

## Six-board spectral-sequence classroom

The auditorium has three independent pairs of counter-moving boards, six surfaces total, each with its own canvas texture. Two depth-separated rails let the boards pass while exchanging upper/lower positions. The sequence writes across the three lower boards, lowers the other boards in turn and preserves the previous work above. Grain stays deterministic rather than flickering. Row-wise reveal preserves real SVG mathematical layout; it is a chalk-writing effect, **not reconstructed human handwriting strokes**.

The chalk tip samples the composed page's actual ink columns, excludes empty trailing columns from its motion, and lifts across gaps/row changes. The texture reveal follows the same ink-only progression. If pixel readback is unavailable it falls back to measured text-row bounds, not the old fixed 1,380-pixel sweep. This remains an ink-guided reveal, not linguistically reconstructed stroke order. The shaft wears from 17 cm down to 4.5 cm while the contact tip remains anchored; short pieces are replaced between pages. At most 64 subtle falling-powder particles are recycled. A tilted felt-faced eraser follows short horizontal runs of occupied 32 × 28 pixel ink tiles, lifting across empty areas. Write and wipe duration are proportional to the measured path cost instead of assigning equal time to every row; short letters or equals signs no longer consume a whole row's time. A single incremental wipe cache avoids replaying all past clipping operations every frame. Recently wiped regions darken slightly and have a local low-resolution roughness-map change, then dry out over 12 seconds. Pause freezes wear/dust/drying; reduced-motion hides tools/powder and clears wet trails. Only changing boards upload color/roughness textures. The six 384 × 160 roughness maps add about 1.9 MiB including mipmaps. These are visual effects rather than simulated chalk adhesion or moisture physics.

`build-chalk-notes.mjs` reads the existing `study/spectral` exports and converts their formulas into 36 local SVG pages using build-only MathJax 3.2.2. Pages cover double complexes, filtrations, convergence, Hodge and Leray; the manifest retains the source TeX and section. They reproduce the exported main formula content with short context, not every proof-dialog derivation or interactive diagram. The classroom links back to the original notebook for full statements, proofs and interactions. No runtime CDN is needed. SVG formula paths avoid depending on installed mathematical fonts; Runtime Chinese captions use locally bundled Ma Shan Zheng; mathematical and Latin runs use a local print face, including Unicode superscripts and inline variables, with complete Chinese/English caption translations for all 36 pages. Language changes rebuild visible pages and ink guides without changing formula TeX or the current page/lift positions. The vector reader follows the same language. Long explanations retain their full text in the reader; physical boards use up to three lines with an ellipsis. See [MathJax server integration](https://docs.mathjax.org/en/v3.2/server/direct.html).

Controls: select any page, previous/next, pause/resume writing, erase/rewrite, focus the lesson camera, toggle the sea-facing blackout shade, and slide or exchange each board pair. Manual lifting pauses writing. Reduced-motion mode displays complete static pages; page controls and direct lifts still work. Hidden tabs do not advance the lesson. Loading failures appear in the classroom status.

### Mobile clarity and rendering budget

Phones no longer automatically select the old 1×, no-MSAA preset. Fine quality allows native pixel density up to 3×, bounded by a 3.5-million-pixel small-screen budget (6 million on larger screens). A 390 × 844 CSS-pixel, 3× device stays at 3× in either orientation. On compact screens with a multisampled native framebuffer, the scene renders directly with native antialiasing and ACES, bypassing bloom/composer passes. If native multisampling is unavailable, the compositor uses hardware-limited MSAA. Flow mode remains an explicit choice; no device frame-rate guarantee is implied. Resize/orientation changes re-evaluate the budget and dispose outdated MSAA attachments. Compact shadow maps use 1024²; readable pixels take priority over bloom.

Portrait teaching frames the active board rather than retreating to fit the full pair. Board headings/body text use 44/36 texture pixels, short formulas have a consistent scale cap, grain is less intrusive, and mipmaps reduce minification shimmer. Only actually changed board textures upload. The optional **高清板书** layer presents local standalone SVG equations and reflowed HTML explanations, independent of the WebGL texture and postprocessing. It opens on small-screen entry to the teaching chapter; closing it is respected for that chapter. Formula size is adjustable (18–36 CSS pixels); long formulas scroll horizontally rather than shrinking illegibly. It shows a complete page while the 3D classroom retains its writing/erasing animation. Full notebook text is retained in this reader even when the physical board uses a shorter text excerpt.

To regenerate with the pinned extracted `mathjax-full` package: `node visuals/math-refuge/build-chalk-notes.mjs /path/to/mathjax-full-3.2.2`. MathJax's license is included under `assets/chalk/`.

### Contemporary techniques considered

Primary documentation reviewed on 2026-09-19:

- [Three.js WebGPURenderer](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGPURenderer.html): modern WebGPU backend with WebGL2 fallback. Not enabled here: this project currently shares pinned Three.js 0.180.0 WebGL addons and planar-reflection materials. Calling this module WebGPU-rendered would be incorrect.
- [Spark Gaussian splat LoD](https://sparkjs.dev/docs/lod-getting-started/): streamed/adaptive splat rendering can support detailed captured environments. No training-ready, geometrically consistent capture or splat asset was supplied, so Gaussian splatting is not used or simulated by ordinary points.
- [three-gpu-pathtracer](https://github.com/gkjohnson/three-gpu-pathtracer): progressive physically based path tracing. Not included in this version: convergence during continuous travel and target-device performance require a separate validation pass. Current lighting is rasterized PBR with planar reflection, not full path tracing/global illumination.

The requested goal “quality and detail no lower than the video” remains **unverified and not claimed achieved**. Matching every reference room and close-up would require more asset detail and visual comparison. Earlier iterations used code checks only; the September 22 teaching and lighting update also receives browser checks.

## Code verification

### Manual camera ownership

Canvas pointer-down takes over the camera in the document capture phase, before OrbitControls processes the gesture. Dragging, multi-touch, pointer cancellation and window blur cannot restart the tour. Drag-generated compatibility clicks on chapter/tour/board-focus controls are suppressed; a fresh button press is accepted immediately. Light/sensitivity sliders are not treated as camera drags. Orbit auto-rotation is explicitly disabled. Chapter buttons select viewpoints without implicitly starting a tour. The teaching chapter tracks the current board after the configured manual-view idle delay. Resuming snapshots camera position, target and field of view, clears residual OrbitControls damping and eases continuously back over 2.2–5.5 seconds. No blackout mask or midpoint teleport is used, and the current chapter's transition fade is suppressed until its next boundary. After manual takeover, only the tour button (or its deliberate Space-key shortcut) starts touring; held-key repeats are ignored. The initial page-load tour behavior is unchanged. Mode labels and canvas diagnostics update immediately on takeover.

## September 22: teaching, local time and smart glass

- Teaching follows the active moving board independently of the tour. Manual camera movement temporarily takes priority; after 20 idle seconds it eases back. The settings slider accepts 5–60 seconds. Holding or cancelling a drag does not unexpectedly restart a tour.
- 36 mathematical units replace 65 isolated-formula pages. `chalk-outline.mjs` records the lecture section references, grouped formulas and distinct bilingual explanations. `chalk-layout.mjs` separates displayed formula rows without splitting fractions; each row finishes before the chalk moves to the next. The repeated byline and artificial “Foundations” numbering have been removed.
- The clock samples the device’s local wall time, including after suspended tabs and midnight. The time slider explicitly enters a frozen preview; the return button restores local-time synchronization. Reduced motion does not stop the real clock.
- Four low-iron smart glass panels replace dense east-wall mullions. Three 8 mm joints and restrained perimeter frames preserve the sea view. The language touch surface is transparent and sits on this glazing, with a quiet cyan selected-language indicator.
- Sculpted bronze/opal pendants, reading bars, soft coves and three separate blackboard wall-wash sources provide layered night illumination. Upstairs rooms now have actual lights as well as visible fixtures. These are artistic real-time lighting settings, not a calibrated illuminance simulation.

- All mathematical expressions, including inline variables, Unicode superscripts and symbols in titles or prose, use print faces in the 3D board, vector reader and generated SVG pages. Chinese prose retains the chalk font.
- Controls are unframed line icons in the upper-right corner, with tooltips and accessible names. Chapter navigation uses a fine active underline.

- Four finite-range, shadow-free warm fill lights simulate reflected room light in the seminar hall, reducing dark seat sides and ceiling/ground contrast at night. The glass language control is a single toggle wholly inside the outer pane, with more than two metres of lateral clearance from the boards.

- Six relevant teaching units now include compact vector diagrams from the notebook conventions: a double complex, total-degree diagonal, column filtration, second-page differential, zeroth page and stable-page diagonal. Formula and diagram ink regions are disjoint; labels remain print mathematics.
- Writing speed is adjustable from 0.25× to 2× independently of touring. Chinese ink contact advances at half the mathematical-ink speed; the chalk shaft tilts down and outward from its anchored upper tip.
- The hall has a bounded diffuse-irradiance fill approximation, in addition to actual lamps, to keep the ceiling, upper boards and chair backs from falling into black. It is an artistic approximation to bounced light, not ray-traced global illumination.
- Continuous thin edge strips follow the union boundaries of platforms and roofs, with room perimeter coves, bridge edges and step nosings. Twenty-six platform lanterns alternate stone niches, bronze bollards and slender poles. Their soft ground pools are shared transparent falloff patches; they do not each add a realtime shadow map.

- Sky scattering and water shading now follow the same day/night factor as the scene clock, so midnight no longer leaves a white horizon and daytime-blue sea behind the room lighting.
