# All-sky Milky Way panorama

Credit: ESO/S. Brunier. Image eso0932a, The Milky Way panorama.
https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/
Downloaded large JPEG (6000 × 3000): https://www.eso.org/public/archives/images/large/eso0932a.jpg

Original JPEG retained. Display changes: sphere mapping, artistic orientation, restrained linear-light exposure, highlight compression and reduced chroma, twilight and cloud attenuation. No claim of a calibrated Shanghai star chart.

Design reference: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, Deep Star Maps 2020, https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/ (reference only; no NASA asset bundled).

2026-09-24: galaxy-diffuse.jpg is a 2048×1024 median-filtered, softly filtered derivative of the credited ESO panorama for diffuse galactic light only. Individual point stars are a deterministic artistic distribution, not a star catalogue.
