Summary

What the program gives

Start on a small lc model

Polarization gives a finite equivariant lift on a normal complex projective input, after iteration.

Repair at each divisorial step

The relative canonical model keeps the program in the lc category. A finite prefix and ample scaling lead to a Mori fibre contraction.

Inspect the torsion endpoint

The canonical class is torsion. The elliptic join shows why the klt model matters for reaching the Q\mathbb QQ-abelian base.

For general int-amplified maps, finite generation is available. The induction also requires an equivariant small lc model on the initial space and on the Mori fibre bases.
