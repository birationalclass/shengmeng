# Native Veronese coefficient charts

Let K be a field, let S be a commutative K-algebra with an N-grading B, and let M be an S-module with compatible K-action. Let D be an integer-indexed family of K-submodules of M satisfying the given graded multiplication law. For q>0 and every integer shift k, the actual native coefficient chart of the Veronese submodule at f in regraded degree d corresponds bijectively to the native chart of M at the same f in original degree q*d, with original shift q*k. The correspondence preserves fraction values and addition and respects the exact degree-zero chart ring equivalence. It commutes with the actual weighted restrictions at f to f*g. No torsion-freeness, flatness or projectivity of M is assumed.
