# Milestone 63 — actual common chart and coordinate-domain injection

The full Linearity Theorem remains unproved.

The homogeneous dehomogenization membership criterion is proved for the original prime ideal on an inhabited standard chart. Original projective surjectivity and total invariance imply that the actual first coordinate form, and hence its actual dehomogenization, do not vanish identically on V. The earlier smooth-principal-open construction therefore supplies an actual smooth source point with nonzero rational-chart denominator, without this condition being supplied separately.

Positive homogeneous degree and actual projective surjectivity imply actual vector-map surjectivity over the complex field by taking a root of the scalar relating representatives. Total invariance consequently gives surjectivity on the actual affine cone. Nullstellensatz then proves contraction of the original ideal under polynomial pullback equals the ideal itself. The actual quotient coordinate-domain endomorphism is constructed and proved injective.

No theorem about all smooth general fibers, their cardinalities, the degree-zero projective function field, global duality lifting or geometric intersection is asserted. Those remain part of the unfinished exact main target.
