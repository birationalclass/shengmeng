# Milestone 62 — actual affine projective geometry and smooth points

The full Linearity Theorem remains unproved.

From the original homogeneous prime ideal and an actual normalized projective point, the affine dehomogenized ideal is proved prime. Its point ideals and their contraction to evaluation kernels are constructed. Total invariance constructs actual affine image points, the pullback point-ideal equality and a positive ideal-power sandwich.

The actual rational-chart local quotient pullback is proved essentially finite type. The local Jacobian-socle result therefore no longer needs this as an independent hypothesis; its smoothness, differential-rank and unramified hypotheses remain explicit.

A finitely presented integral algebra over a perfect field has a nonzero smooth principal open, constructed from the generic point. A nontrivial finite-type algebra over an algebraically closed field has an actual rational point by the maximal-ideal form of the Nullstellensatz. Together they produce an actual smooth complex point on any inhabited standard affine chart of the original projective variety, including a prescribed nonempty principal open. Actual finite smooth families construct common ambient and formal normal coordinates.

Still required: constructing the general projective etale fibers and their cardinalities, identifying differential rank with geometric dimension, uniform global Serre/duality lifting, geometric Cartier/Bezout comparison, and the complete original target proof. Helper counts do not certify these obligations.
