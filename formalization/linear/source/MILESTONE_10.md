# Milestone 10 — actual derivative Jacobian and scalar socle

The full Linearity Theorem is **unproved**. This milestone proves a necessary
local algebra input; it does not prove the relative/arbitrary-parameter or
global geometric theorem.

For a characteristic-zero field K and a regular sequence H of n+1
zero-constant multivariable power series, with its actual quotient A assumed
Artinian and finite-dimensional over K, `JacobianNonzero.lean` proves:

* the actual class of det(∂H_i/∂X_j) is nonzero;
* x annihilates the nilradical iff x is a K-scalar multiple of this class;
* the annihilator ideal is the principal ideal generated by this class.

No pairing, socle generator, determinant comparison, or Jacobian nonzero
statement is assumed in those conclusions. The proof constructs a polynomial
unit change, a universal difference matrix in two sets of variables, and an
actual diagonal element in A ⊗ A. Its right reduction is a previously proved
nonzero coefficient determinant. The perfect-pairing diagonal argument shows
that its coefficient is a unit; multiplication identifies its image with the
actual derivative determinant. The characteristic-zero trace calculation
proves nonvanishing, and the pairing proves scalar socle generation.

documentation for tensor quotients, flatness, duality, and tensor finiteness:

Those APIs provide actual objects and maps but the inspected declarations
